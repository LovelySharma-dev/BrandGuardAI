## Critical planning findings (read first)

1. **Name collision risk (Verified evidence):** A commercial product already markets itself as [BrandGuard AI](https://brandguardai.io/) for brand/legal content governance (accessed 2026-10-20). Retain “BrandGuard AI” only as a provisional label; rename before public launch or demo branding.
2. **Category is crowded (Verified evidence of competitor existence, not of market share):** Enterprise tools such as Writer and Acrolinx / Markup AI already sell brand + compliance review. Differentiation cannot be “AI checks guidelines.”
3. **Official challenge packet not independently verified:** This package follows the team-supplied Round 1 brief. Public web search did not surface a matching official “brand-governance Round 1” PDF from Inkloom on 2026-10-20. Prior public Inkloom-sponsored hackathon posts focused on brand *creation*, not governance. Treat organiser form fields, deadlines, and exact PDF requirements as **Unresolved** until the team attaches the official packet.
4. **No functional build in Round 1 planning:** Architecture and docs only until the permitted development window opens.
